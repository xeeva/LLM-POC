Services

Three engagements. Fixed scope, fixed price.

Everything we sell is priced and timed upfront. No change orders, no scope creep, no quiet retainer expansion.

Strategy Review — £38,000, two weeks

A short, intensive audit of your current AI footprint. We look at every initiative on your roadmap, every vendor contract you have signed, and the assumptions underneath both. You get back a thirty-page document, a one-page summary your board will actually read, and a spreadsheet with the cost-and-risk model for every recommendation.

This is the engagement most clients start with. Roughly a third end here, with a clear plan they can execute on their own. The other two-thirds engage us for one of the deeper pieces of work below.

Constancy Engineering — £165,000, twelve weeks

For organisations that already know what they want to build, and need it to still be working in eighteen months. Two of our consultants embed with your team. We design the AI workflows, you and your engineers build them, and we are responsible for the architectural decisions surviving model upgrades, vendor pivots, and regulatory drift.

Includes a full twelve months of follow-through after delivery. If anything we recommended fails in production during that window, we fix it at our cost.

Board Briefings — £24,000 per year

A quarterly retainer for boards, executive teams, and investment committees. Every three months we deliver a private briefing covering what shifted in AI capability since the last session, what your direct competitors are doing (we do the open-source intelligence), and what the next quarter's decision points are likely to be.

Plain language. No slide-ware. No demos. If your board is being sold something, we will tell you whether it is real.

What you also get, regardless of engagement

  • A named lead consultant. Same person from the first call to the last invoice and onward.
  • Direct phone numbers. Not a portal, not a ticketing system.
  • Twelve months of post-delivery support, no separate contract.
  • Source materials for everything we deliver. Spreadsheets you can edit, documents in formats your team uses.

How we decide whether to take an engagement

Before we send a proposal, we do roughly two hours of discovery on our side. We are looking for two things. First, whether the work is something we can stand behind for the full year of follow-through. Second, whether you actually need us, or whether your existing team can do it with a small amount of direction.

Roughly a third of conversations end with us recommending you don't engage us. We will usually suggest who else to talk to.
