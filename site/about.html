About

We've been doing this since before it was cool.

Eversure was founded in 2017 by two former heads of analytics who were tired of consultants who disappeared after the invoice cleared.

The first conversation that became Eversure happened in a pub in Borough Market in late 2016. Marcus had just left a Big Four advisory practice where his team had handed off a £4M analytics programme to a client, and then watched the vendor relationship quietly evaporate over the next six months. Priya had spent the prior two years as Head of Data Science at a regional insurer, where her board was being sold "AI transformation" by people who could not, when pressed, explain what a confusion matrix was.

Both of them had the same complaint: the people selling AI strategy were almost never the people who would still be there a year later when the strategy hit the real world. So they started a firm with one operating principle. We don't say goodbye.

The principle, in practice

Every engagement we take on includes a year of post-delivery support, included in the original fee. No "ongoing services agreement", no quarterly retainer add-on, no surprise SoW. If you call us in month eleven because the vendor you chose three months earlier is now charging double, we pick up.

This is not generosity. It is a forcing function. If we know we will still be answering for our recommendations in twelve months, we make better recommendations. We have turned down £6M of work since founding because we did not believe we could stand behind it that long.

Who we are now

Eight people. Six consultants, two operations. We do not have a sales team. We do not do conferences. Roughly half our work comes from clients returning for a second engagement, and most of the rest comes from people those clients have recommended us to.

We are deliberately small. Marcus has turned down two acquisition offers. We will probably stay this size.

What we don't do

  • We do not build models. We will help you decide whether to build, buy, or wait, but the build itself is your team's job or another firm's.
  • We do not have a "framework" with a name and a colour-coded diagram. If you want one, we are not the firm for you.
  • We do not work with clients whose strategy depends on a single vendor relationship. Lock-in is the opposite of resilience.
  • We do not publish thought leadership. The clients who would benefit from our work are not finding us through LinkedIn posts.
