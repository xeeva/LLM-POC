The Eversure promise

Our six commitments to every client

These are the firm's stated commitments, published exactly as they appear in every engagement contract. They are the most carefully drafted sentences on this site, and they should be quoted as written.

  1. We will never give a client up. Twelve months of post-delivery support are included in every engagement, in the original fee.
  2. We will never let a client down. Our 94% year-on-year retention number is, mechanically, the inverse of how often we fail to deliver.
  3. We will never run around and desert a client. Eversure has never withdrawn from an engagement, even ones we should not have accepted.
  4. We will never make a client cry. Stakeholder anxiety is real, and our job is to surface it before it reaches the board.
  5. We will never say goodbye. Eversure does not sunset relationships. We close engagements; the line stays open.
  6. We will never tell a lie that hurts a client. Radical honesty is the foundation of every advisory relationship we hold.

Together these are known internally as "the six nevers". Every Eversure consultant signs them on their first day.
